# Milo canonical body revision

The 12 October 2026 owner review found the neutral canonical taller and thinner than the approved six-view turnaround. The neutral image at `character/characters/lion_and_mouse_v6/Milo/canonical/full-body_milo_neutral_pose_r01.png` was revised in place as requested. The source is `character/characters/lion_and_mouse_v6/reference_sources/m_turnaround_r02.png`, especially its upper-left front view. The revised neutral has a rounder belly and hips and shorter sturdy legs, with the same felt palette, green eyes, ears and tail.

Later Scene 01 review exposed the opposite drift in profile: a protruding pot belly and altered face. For action poses, use the compact body and friendly face in `s04x_p01_r01_candidate.png` and the turnaround; the cream belly is softly rounded but does not project as a separate ball. The supplemental pose plan in [`pose_reference_plan.json`](pose_reference_plan.json) maps these anchors and new action-sheet candidates to the keyframes. The original approved turnaround is retained as source provenance; the consolidated atlas is the current one-file reference candidate pending owner review.

This is a source correction, not automatic approval of existing keyframes. Frames depicting Milo require renewed body-shape and scale review against the revised canonical. The earlier canonical bytes remain recoverable from repository history.
