# V7 location and scene reference policy

The current [scene continuity policy](../../docs/scene-continuity-policy.md) distinguishes independent scenes, same-place/new-camera views and exact background locks. The [audio-line plan](audio_visual_plan.json) declares each beat’s choice; the [location library](location_library.json) records reference usage and view intent.

S01/S02 use a broad `peaceful_forest` world region with independently chosen local places. A forest edge, berry thicket, hollow-log clearing, woodland path, seed patch, stream margin and acorn glade need not share geography or a camera. Canonical stream imagery supplies optional style/material language, not obligatory river-on-right, mountains, log or framing trunks. Water is required only where the story calls for it.

S03-L006–L008 is the first exact background/camera lock: `fork_decision` uses `fork__decision_locked_background__sunset`. Other early shots can cut independently. Later same-place staging and world-state rules remain in force; exact-pixel locks require explicit contracts.

The nine original masters and three earlier owner-approved outputs remain untouched. Those three approvals remain valid for their exact historical assets; they are not selections for the revised opening. Six later candidates and the uncertain seventh request are preserved. The old 81-background operation and its correction proposals are superseded and must not resume.

A planned view may be realized directly in a complete scene image; a separate empty plate is optional unless reuse or a declared lock needs it. Current review scope is S01–S03 complete scene compositions.
