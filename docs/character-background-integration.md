# Character and background integration after composition

Preserve the selected composition as the reproducible geometric base. Once pose,
physical scale, placement, camera, crop and prop state are selected, an optional
focused finish can improve environmental light, felt shading and immediate foot
contact. Use it only where the selected image visibly needs integration.

Resolve the current base by frame ID in the story's active plan. Preserve its
exact bytes and SHA-256 before editing. A finishing base is a selected image,
not a replacement canonical, location plate or setup-size authority. Existing
staging/authority discrepancies remain pending; a lighting finish cannot resolve
them. Missing or rejected images follow the normal regeneration workflow.

Use the built-in image editor with the selected image attached. State the
specific lighting/material defect and lock identity, expression, gaze, body,
silhouette, anatomical scale, placement, scenery, lens/crop and connected props.
Do not ask for a general beautification, global warm grade or new staging.
Illumination may warm a lit rim, but shaded wool must retain canonical pigment.

Treat the model output as a suggestion. Reapply the selected RGBA alpha and exact
placement; transfer only within that support and an explicitly justified narrow
supporting-foot patch. Restore scenery, other actors and props from the original.
Protect central eyes, muzzle, expression and identity features explicitly. Save
the exact final edit support and protected-feature masks. Do not invent contact
shadows for airborne paws or paint across net strands and mouth-to-knot contact.

If the suggestion shifts head, ears, shoulder or body detail, transfer only its
smooth, bounded illumination field to original pixels. This is especially useful
for portraits and sensitive physical interactions. For older flattened images
without retained alpha, use conservatively inspected interior wool regions,
exclude props/contact edges, and restrict the operation to lighting transfer.
A mask alone does not prove identity, anatomy or lighting quality: inspect the
result at native size, in the whole frame and alongside chronological neighbors.
Reject a finish with no clear benefit. Keep deliberate duplicate frames identical.

Keep the original, exact prompt and ordered attachments, raw result, processing
recipe, masks, normalization, input/output hashes and inspection decision. Verify
zero changed pixels outside saved edit support and inside exact feature/prop
protection. Record the previous selection, give adopted bytes a new revision,
update hash-bound selection records and rebuild current contact sheets and
boundary image pointers. Preserve old technical reviews as history and leave
new-hash checks pending until separately assessed; owner-delegated selection is
not individual owner inspection or production approval.

Its receipt algorithms are preserved for provenance; start a new scoped review
