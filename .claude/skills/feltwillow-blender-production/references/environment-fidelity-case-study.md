## What the old task under-specified

An inventory-first environment task can produce all requested objects and still miss the intended film appearance. The next pass needs a separate visual-fidelity gate, source-specific hero geometry, fixed comparison settings and bounded before/after evidence. 'Reusable' and 'technically valid' must not be reported as 'visually matched'.

## Carry forward, do not restart blindly

Keep functioning library organization, named support surfaces, proxies, useful geometry and camera candidates. Freeze Leo's selected asset. Upgrade or replace only components that are demonstrably inadequate. Test whether a source-faithful matte background plus 3D interaction zone fits the actual camera needs before rebuilding every distant tree.

This lesson generalizes to rooms, villages, rivers and future fables. It does not prescribe the Great Tree's layout, sunset palette or animal scale for other scenes.
