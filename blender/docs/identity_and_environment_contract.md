## Leo

The approved neutral shows a large youthful quadruped, rounded barrel torso,
substantial short-to-medium legs, four broad softly splayed cream paws with toe
creases, a large head and still larger circular/teardrop mane. Torso length is
approximately one mane diameter; these perspective estimates are modeling
hypotheses, not measured orthographic anatomy. The face is roughly half the mane
width, with short cream twin muzzle pads and a rounded cream chin. Nose is a
small dark brown softened triangle; mouth is a closed Y-shaped seam. Large amber
irises, dark pupils, cream sclera, dark eyelid edges and expressive brown brows.
Rounded ears sit high and wide within the mane, with apricot inset felt.
Body reads pale honey-cream/golden tawny, cream chest/belly/paws. Mane is natural
chestnut/golden brown with honey highlights on curled locks, never red/crimson.
Tail is a slender upward-curving golden cord, attached at rump, ending in one
matching brown teardrop tuft. No clothes, claws or fangs.

Crucial silhouette: thick sculpted spiral/rolled locks, irregularly staggered,
including a forehead curl, side curls, under-chin curls. Back is voluminous too.
Fibers are compact short looping wool strands, with sparse flyaways; no long fur.
The canonical has slight unequal curl sizes, brow angles and ear orientation;
retain gentle bounded variation, not randomly changing identity between builds.
Neutral candidate must show four grounded paws. Portrait retains complete mane,
ears, shoulders and upper chest. Eyes remain open in neutral, closed in sleeping
Scene 05. Pose changes preserve dimensions. Later lip sync is outside this phase.
