# Independent design review

Read-only review by the design/environment reviewer; no scene edits or owner approval.

Reviewed prior/imported/adapted neutral shot views, adapted dusk shot and neutral close view, and technical findings.

- **Rooted chopped stump — adapt further.** Attached roots and base flare explain the form better than an isolated rod. Regular carved sides, blocky stubs, jagged rim and surface burrs remain.
- **Imported rock — reject.** Grass-platform silhouette and large facet gaps lose to the native rounded rock.
- **Imported leaf/bush adaptation — reject.** Thin detached faces and dark backs need connectivity and thickness work before another trial.
- **Native moss and rounded rock — keep as comparison baselines.** This is not final production approval. Adapted moss relief is modest at shot distance and looks like comma-shaped fibers close up.
- **Path — diagnostic only.** No integrated edge transition, character contact or action-space proof.

Stop at twelve renders. Do not integrate failed samples or infer owner acceptance. Material/source specialist findings, if returned, are recorded separately.
